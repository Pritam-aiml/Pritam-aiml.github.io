Directions to reach the venue

By train

Sealdah to Baruipur Junction

  1. Board a local train from Sealdah Railway Station to Baruipur Junction.
  2. After exiting Platform No. 1, take an auto on the Baruipur–Garia route, heading towards Garia.
  3. Get down at Spandan Medicare, near Propel Petrol Pump.

From Baruipur Junction 25 min

By metro

Kavi Nazrul Metro Station, Garia

  1. Take the metro to Kavi Nazrul Metro Station (Garia Bazar).
  2. After exiting the station, take the left-side exit gate.
  3. Board an auto or bus on the Garia–Baruipur route, heading towards Baruipur.
  4. Get down at Spandan Medicare, near Propel Petrol Pump.

From Kavi Nazrul Metro 30 min
