About

I work at the intersection of experimental metallurgy and data-driven modelling. My research couples microstructure–property correlation through advanced characterization with machine-learning approaches to predict phases, crystal structures, and mechanical behaviour of complex alloy systems. Alongside the lab, I design and validate research-grade computational materials science problems for training and evaluating frontier large language models.
